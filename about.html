About Buildora

Making it easier to get quality building materials.

Our Story

Buildora started with a simple idea: buying building materials shouldn't mean spending a whole day moving between hardware shops.

We bring cement, steel, timber, roofing, plumbing, electrical supplies and tools into one online store. Homeowners, fundis and contractors can compare prices, order online and get materials delivered straight to site. That saves time and cuts transport costs.

Every product we stock is sourced from certified manufacturers, so you can build with confidence.

Our Vision

To be the foundation behind every great build in East Africa.

We see a future where anyone, from a family building their first home to a contractor raising a high-rise, can get genuine materials at a fair price, without the hassle.

Our Mission

Quality materials, fair prices, delivered on time, every time.

We make building simpler by supplying certified materials, delivering them reliably to every site, and giving honest advice from people who understand construction.

Build better. Build with Buildora.
